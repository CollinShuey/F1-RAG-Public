F1 Regs AI

An AI-powered chatbot that answers questions about FIA Formula 1 regulations using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). Built from scratch with Python and ChromaDB, with no frameworks, to deeply understand the RAG pipeline, then refactored with LangChain for comparison.

Why F1 regulations?

LLMs weren't trained on the current regulations, so they simply don't have this information, and the data they were trained on is usually a year or two out of date. That makes them unreliable on detailed questions about specific clauses, minimum car weights, or cost cap exceptions. RAG solves this by grounding the model in the actual source documents, the exact use case it was designed for.

How it saves tokens

The regulations run to hundreds of pages. Feeding all of that to the model on every question would be slow and expensive. Instead, the documents are split into chunks and stored as vectors in a vector database. When you ask something, a nearest-neighbor (KNN) search retrieves only the handful of most relevant passages and sends just those to the model. That keeps the context small, cuts token usage and cost, and keeps every answer grounded in the exact rules that matter.
